# Changelog

All notable changes to the FlockSense platform will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Release
### Added
- Core modular architecture with Flutter 3.x and Dart.
- Feature-first domain modules:
  - Farm & Shed Management
  - Daily Entry & Telemetry Logging
  - Automated Growth Analytics & FCR Calculator
  - Health & Risk Escalation Engine (Firebase Cloud Functions)
  - Medical, Vaccine, and Inventory Tracking
  - Commercial Bird Sales & Financial Ledger
  - Automated PDF/Excel/CSV Report Generator
  - Dark mode and animated analytics visualizations
